Our mission

Make mindful food use easier in everyday life.

Food waste and unhealthy eating habits often begin with the same everyday problem: losing track of what we have, what needs attention, and what can be used next. ThinkEat Foundation focuses on practical ways to close that gap.

Meet the ThinkEat app.

The ThinkEat app is a practical household tool designed to help people understand what food they have, what needs attention, and how their grocery choices turn out.

  • Keep track of food across the fridge, freezer, and pantry.
  • Notice items before they are forgotten or expire.
  • Record whether food was consumed, donated, or thrown away.
  • Build awareness of personal food-use habits over time.
